PTD inference for MiniMax-H3

The upstream MiniMax-H3 inference code comes from MiniMax. MiniMax-H3 components
used through Hugging Face Diffusers retain their MiniMax and Hugging Face
copyright notices in the source files.

EPFL VITA Lab made minor PTD-specific adaptations to support the PTD checkpoints,
including hop-routed adapters, sampling recipes, checkpoint loading and condition
caching. The repository also includes supporting scripts and tests for these
adaptations. EPFL VITA Lab's notices apply to these changes and supporting code;
the upstream notices remain applicable to the upstream components.

The PTD checkpoints in https://huggingface.co/epfl-vita/PTD-weights were trained by
EPFL VITA Lab based on the paper "Parametric Trajectory Distillation for Few-Step
Video Generation" (arXiv:2610.11498): https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11498.
